The Evening Bath Protocol.

Dissolve two cups of unrefined carnallite crystals into thermal water. As your body submerges, transdermal magnesium absorption acts directly on the neuromuscular system, calming cellular stress and accelerating moisture retention.

  • 01 Prepare unboiled thermal water precisely at body temperature (37°C / 98.6°F).

  • 03 Immerse for 20 minutes without rinsing with tap water to seal the mineral veil.
